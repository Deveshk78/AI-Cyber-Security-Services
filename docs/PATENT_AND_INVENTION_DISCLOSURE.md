## Executive summary

This disclosure describes a portfolio of inventions and technical concepts related to AI security operations, governance workflows, and evidence-bound control automation. The invention combines policy-aware AI review, risk scoring, red-team simulation, compliance intelligence, and automated human escalation into a unified product concept for secure AI adoption.

## Core invention concepts

### 1. Dual-layer AI protection architecture

A model execution framework in which an untrusted or permissive AI layer performs extraction, transformation, or interpretation tasks, while a separate trusted control layer validates outputs and blocks unsafe or unauthorized actions.

### 2. Evidence-bound security reasoning

Security operations workflows that convert raw events and telemetry into structured evidence bundles, assigning a cryptographic or traceability anchor to each decision and preserving chain-of-custody for policy enforcement.

### 3. AI red-team simulation and governance mapping

A simulation framework that generates adversarial prompts, scores behavior against risk taxonomies, and routes findings through a governance model for severity classification and remediation support.

### 4. Semantic compliance intelligence

A policy matching layer using semantic similarity methods to align AI assets, controls, and risk scenarios with governance requirements such as safety, privacy, and compliance obligations.

### 5. Human-in-the-loop policy gate

A system in which high-risk automated actions are prevented unless a human reviewer confirms the decision, thereby reducing unsafe automation caused by hallucination, misclassification, or prompt exploitation.

## Innovation significance

The overall innovation is the combination of AI red teaming, semantic policy intelligence, evidence-backed security decisioning, and compliance-oriented governance into a single operational model for enterprise AI assurance.

## Potential application areas

- enterprise AI governance

## Prior art context

Existing systems typically emphasize model monitoring, LLM safety layer controls, or cybersecurity automation independently. This invention differentiates itself by integrating these elements into a unified risk and compliance execution model with traceable evidence and governance workflows.

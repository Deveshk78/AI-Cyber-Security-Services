## Overview

This Request for Proposal outlines the scope for AI security consulting, prototype development, and governance design services for an enterprise AI assurance initiative. The offering combines technical architecture, AI risk analysis, and compliance readiness to help organizations adopt AI securely.

## Objectives

- assess and document AI security risks
- prototype AI guardrail and governance workflows
- evaluate red-team and adversarial testing scenarios
- propose governance and compliance controls
- deliver a pilotable AI security operations framework

## Proposed deliverables

1. AI security architecture assessment
2. Risk taxonomy and threat modeling workshops
3. Red-team simulation framework and evaluation plan
4. Governance and compliance control mapping
5. Documentation package including README, compliance, thesis, and roadmap
6. Executive summary with strategic recommendations

## Scope details

### Technical scope

- secure AI workflow design
- adversarial prompt testing
- guardrail logic and policy enforcement design
- model governance and review process architecture
- security telemetry and incident handling design

### Business scope

- executive risk assessment
- governance program strategy
- compliance alignment review
- stakeholder readiness and rollout planning

## Response requirements

Interested organizations or stakeholders should provide:

- organization profile and relevant AI maturity context
- expected deployment environment
- data sensitivity and governance constraints
- desired timeline and pilot scope
- key stakeholders and review authorities

## Commercial statement

This RfP is intended for planning and benchmarking purposes. Final pricing, timelines, and production deployment scope will be defined through a signed statement of work and legal review.
